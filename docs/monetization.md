# Monetization — thresholds (v0.1.0)

ClipForge's monetization tracker shows **progress toward thresholds** per
platform. It never promises earnings, RPMs, or payouts — platforms publish
no fixed per-view rates, and reused / templated content can be
demonetized even after thresholds are met.

Thresholds below are the research-report values (20 Nov 2026). Re-verify
on the platform's own pages before quoting them to users.

## YouTube Partner Program (YPP)

| Tier | Subs | Watch hours / 12 mo **or** Shorts views / 90 d |
|---|---|---|
| Early (fan funding + Shopping) | 500 | 3,000 hrs **or** 3M Shorts views |
| Full (ads + Premium) | 1,000 | 4,000 hrs **or** 10M Shorts views |
| **Full — new applicants from 1 Feb 2027** | 1,000 | **8,000 hrs or 20M Shorts views** |

- ⚠️ **Feb 2027 doubling flag:** new applicants from **1 Feb 2027** need
  double the watch hours / Shorts views (8,000 hrs / 20M views). Subs
  unchanged at 1,000. Existing applicants must accept the new YPP terms
  by 31 Jan 2027. ClipForge flags any account whose application date is
  on/after 2027-02-01 and applies the doubled thresholds.
- Only **public** Shorts in the Shorts Feed count; Shorts watch time does
  **not** count toward the 4,000 hrs.
- Shorts ad revenue is pooled, music costs deducted, **45% to the creator**.
  Reported RPM $0.01–$0.06 / 1k views (vs 55% for long-form).
- Reused / inauthentic content = no monetization even if thresholds are
  met (see [compliance.md](compliance.md)).

## TikTok Creator Rewards Program

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Followers | 10,000 |
| Views | 100,000 / 30 days |
| Account | Personal (not Business / government), 18+ (19 in KR), good standing |
| Countries | US, UK, DE, FR, JP, KR, MX, BR only |
| Per video | Original, **≥1 min**, posted after joining, ≥1,000 qualified For You views (unique FYP view, >5 s watched) |

- Payout: reported $0.40–$1.00 / 1k qualified views (sources range
  $0.01–$1.20 — no published RPM). Paid on the 15th monthly.
- Minimum payout: **$50 per the terms; $10 per one help page — VERIFY_LIVE**
  before telling a user which applies.
- **Design implication:** short clips optimised for reach earn $0 in
  Rewards. ClipForge generates a ≥60 s TikTok variant when the user wants
  Rewards eligibility. Duets / Stitches / Photo Mode / ads / sponsored
  content are excluded.

## Instagram

There is **no per-view Reels pay**.

| Path | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Gifts | 500 followers, pro account, 18+, payout account, eligible country — $0.01 per star, paid monthly |
| Subscriptions | 10,000 followers, pro account, 18+ — creator-set price, ~70% yr 1 / ~85% after (app-store fees apply) |
| Bonuses | Invite-only, JP/KR/US only |

- ⚠️ **Reels Play bonus ended 31 Aug 2025 — do not promise it.** Earnings
  now come via gifts / subs / brand deals, not views.
- Without a payout account, earnings accrue only up to $500/tool and
  $1,500 across 3 tools.

## Facebook — Content Monetization Program (CMP)

CMP unified the old In-stream Ads, Ads on Reels, and Performance Bonus
(all stopped earning 31 Aug 2025).

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | Page (not personal profile) or pro-mode profile; **10,000 followers** per CMP guide |
| Legacy figures — VERIFY_LIVE | Some 2026 guides cite 5,000 followers + 60,000 min watched / 60 d or 5 active Reels / 30 d for legacy in-stream / Reels paths — check Business Suite → Monetization Eligibility |
| Stars | 500 followers + 1 live in 60 d; $0.01 each, $100 minimum |
| Subscriptions | 10,000 followers or 250+ returning viewers; ~70/85% as IG |
| Qualified views | Repeats excluded; views <5 s excluded; Reel must be ≥10 s to earn |

- No published RPM; creator reports $0.02–$0.30 / 1k Reels plays
  (long-form $2–$10 / 1k), ~55/45 split reported.
- Duplicate / reused content = reduced distribution + loss of
  monetization (Jul 2025).

## What the tracker shows

Per account × platform: current subs/followers, watch hours, views in the
relevant window, % toward each threshold, which thresholds are met, and —
for YouTube — whether the Feb 2027 doubling applies. Nothing else. No
earnings estimates, no "you will earn $X" copy anywhere in the product.
